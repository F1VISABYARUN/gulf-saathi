# Gulf Saathi: demo video script (3 minutes)

For Claude Design. Each scene lists what is on screen, the English voice-over (VO),
and the call audio to use. Telugu call audio plays with English subtitles.

**Honesty rules for the video (judges will check):**
- Every call shown is a real Bolna call, recorded on our test phone, with a made-up
  worker. Say so once on screen: "Real calls. Test worker. No real person's data."
- Never say Saathi "files a complaint", "rescues" or "connects to the embassy". It
  prepares a complaint-ready packet and alerts the team, who contact the embassy.
- SMS is shown on the dashboard, not sent (Plivo not set up). Say "SMS prepared".
- Do not name real victims. Use "real cases reported in Telangana follow this pattern".

---

## Scene 1: The problem (0:00 – 0:20)

**On screen:** dark background. A phone screen with a shaky selfie-video frame
(illustration, not a real person). Text appears line by line:
"Promised: cleaner." / "Visa: agricultural worker." / "Reality: herding goats in the desert."
Then: "Today, help starts when the video goes viral."

**VO:** "A worker from Telangana is promised a cleaner's job in the Gulf. His visa says
something else. He lands, and ends up herding goats in the desert. Today, the system
usually finds out only when his video goes viral. Inspired by Aadujeevitham, we built
Gulf Saathi, so nobody has to wait for that video."

**Title card:** "Gulf Saathi · గల్ఫ్ సాథి: a Telugu voice line that checks every step of the Gulf journey."

---

## Scene 2: Before paying (0:20 – 1:05)

**On screen:** the public home page (gulf-saathi-psi.vercel.app), click "Get a call
from Saathi", then a phone ringing. Split screen: phone call on the left, subtitles on the right.
Small badge: "Real call · test worker".

**Call audio (record: Call A, below):**
1. Saathi: "చెప్పండి, ఏం జరిగింది?" / *"Tell me, what happened?"*
2. Caller: agent name + district → Saathi: *"This name was not found in the government licensed list. Ask for the licence number before paying."*
3. Caller: "He is asking 1 lakh." → Saathi: *"That is ₹64,600 over the legal limit of ₹35,400. Don't pay it."*
4. Caller gives the agent's phone number → *"One minute, checking"* →
   Saathi: *"This number has come up in another Saathi case; that worker had serious
   problems after travelling. This is not proof, but please don't pay any more until a
   caseworker checks."*

**On-screen callouts** (one per step): "Licensed list: 2,447 MEA agents (8 Apr 2026)" →
"Legal fee check" → "**Saathi Intelligence**: same phone number, another case".

**VO (over step 4):** "This is Saathi Intelligence. The same agent phone number appeared
in another worker's case. Saathi warns the next worker before he pays, without
naming anyone, and without calling anyone a criminal. A link is a lead for review, not proof."

---

## Scene 3: Before flying (1:05 – 1:25)

**On screen:** dashboard, "Register worker" form filled for the test worker, then the
case page: red flags and the red banner "Tell the worker: do not pay more or travel
until this is sorted out", and the Promise vs reality table (Cleaner / Agricultural worker).

**VO:** "Before he flies, a helper registers him. Promised job: cleaner. The visa says
agricultural worker. Saathi flags it at once: don't pay more, don't travel until this is
sorted out. His family gets one Telugu SMS with his case number."

---

## Scene 4: Arrival day (1:25 – 2:05)

**On screen:** case page → "Call now: arrival check" → phone rings. Subtitles.
Then the case page refreshes: badge turns **CRITICAL**, reasons listed, and the red box
"Alert the Indian Embassy now" with the prepared message (name, place, what happened).

**Call audio (record: Call B):**
1. Saathi: *"Namaskaram Mallayya garu. Did you reach Dubai? Who picked you up, AI Noor Cleaning?"*
2. Worker: *"No, someone else took me."*
3. Saathi: *"I understand. You are not alone. Your case is URGENT and the Saathi team is
   alerting the Indian Embassy now. If you are still inside the airport, stay inside, go
   to the help desk or airport police..."*
4. Worker: *"How do I find the embassy number?"* → Saathi: *"You don't need to search.
   Our team is informing the embassy with your details."*
5. Saathi: *"Is there any building, shop or mosque near you?"*

**VO:** "On arrival day, Saathi calls him. A stranger picked him up. Fixed rules, not
the AI's opinion, mark it CRITICAL. Saathi doesn't hand him a phone number to search
for. It tells him what to do right now, and the team gets a ready message for the embassy,
with where he is and what happened."

---

## Scene 5: The help line (2:05 – 2:25)

**On screen:** phone call (record: Call C), subtitles; then a new help-line case on the dashboard.

**Call audio:** worker abroad: *"I'm in Dubai. They took my passport. I want to come back."*
Saathi waits, then: *"You are not alone. Your case is urgent..."*, one landmark
question ("Deira, Dubai"), case number read letter by letter.

**VO:** "Workers already stuck abroad can reach Saathi too. It listens first, registers the
case in the first minute, and asks only what helps someone find him."

---

## Scene 6: For the government (2:25 – 2:45)

**On screen:** Agents page, "Saathi Intelligence: repeated across cases": a cluster
showing "Seen in 3 cases · 2 at risk · agent districts: Jagtial, Nizamabad". Then the
"Download English case packet" file opening.

**VO:** "For TOMCOM and the state, every case becomes structured evidence: a complaint-ready
packet for MADAD, the embassy or Pravasi Prajavani, and patterns across cases: the same
agent phone, the same payment account, the same sponsor. Leads for trained investigators.
Not a blacklist."

---

## Scene 7: Close (2:45 – 3:00)

**On screen:** three words appear: "Before they pay. When they land. Until they're safe."
Then: "Built on Bolna · Sarvam · Cartesia" and the URL.
Small text: "Saathi cannot rescue anyone or file a case by itself. It makes sure the right
people know, with the facts, before the video."

**VO:** "Gulf Saathi doesn't replace the embassy. It makes sure nobody has to go viral to be heard."
